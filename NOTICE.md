# Source and scientific attribution

This package extracts the typed energy-depletion guidance implementation from
[SpaceAGORA.jl](https://github.com/Space-FALCON-Lab/SpaceAGORA.jl). The original
MIT license and Space-FALCON Lab copyright notice are retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
The extraction preserves the contribution history in the linked core repository;
a new repository does not imply sole authorship of the algorithms.

The scientific formulations are described by Giusy Falcone and Zachary R.
Putnam (2022), and by Nihal Simha, Evan Yu, and Giusy Falcone (2026). Full
references and the distinction between those publications and the current
experimental implementation appear in [Scientific status](docs/scientific_status.md).
Attribution to a publication does not establish reproduction of its figures.
