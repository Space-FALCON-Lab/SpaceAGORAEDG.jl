# Source and scientific attribution

This package contains the typed energy-depletion guidance implementation
extracted from [SpaceAGORA.jl](https://github.com/Space-FALCON-Lab/SpaceAGORA.jl).
The original MIT license and Space-FALCON Lab copyright notice are kept in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). The contribution history stays in the SpaceAGORA repository;
a separate repository does not imply sole authorship of the algorithms.

The scientific formulations are described by Giusy Falcone and Zachary R.
Putnam (2022), and by Nihal Simha, Evan Yu, and Giusy Falcone (2026). Full
references and the distinction between those publications and the current
experimental implementation are in [Scientific status](docs/scientific_status.md).
Citing a publication does not mean this package reproduces its figures.
